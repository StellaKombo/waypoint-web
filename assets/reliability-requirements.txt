# Reliability requirements from developer feedback

Research snapshot: 11 October 2026. This is a representative, source-linked requirements review, not an exhaustive audit of every Maestro issue or a claim of lower flakiness. GitHub reports are user reports in particular environments; Reddit comments are qualitative feedback. Status may change after this snapshot. We have not independently reproduced the reported Maestro bugs.

Maestro already supports mobile/web testing and an official MCP integration. See its [current product page](https://maestro.dev/). Waypoint's goals are approachable authoring, explicit local session control, bounded operations, and clear evidence. Comparative claims require matched real-device and browser benchmarks.

## Release rule

Each pain point needs a reproducible fixture, expected outcome and regression before a mitigation is considered implemented. A passed mock or protocol test demonstrates the contract, not a real-device reliability advantage. Keep infrastructure failures, blocked/manual actions, failed expectations and untested steps distinct. Do not silently retry clicks to hide a failure or treat a changed screenshot as proof of the intended outcome.

## Comparative benchmark still required

Use consented staging apps and identical scenarios on identical devices/browser versions. Record setup time, first-run success, repeated-run failure rate, p50/p95 latency, timeout recovery, wrong-target incidents and evidence usefulness. Include animated modals, delayed rendering, nested scrolling, duplicate names, permission handoffs, WebViews, Unicode text, navigation and interrupted runs. Publish both tools' versions, sample counts, environments and unsupported cases. Until then, do not advertise that Waypoint beats Maestro.

## Not every historical issue remains open

The researched Xcode startup report [#2932](https://github.com/mobile-dev-inc/Maestro/issues/2932) is closed with a linked fix, and [#3477](https://github.com/mobile-dev-inc/Maestro/issues/3477) is a duplicate. Do not reuse closed complaints as evidence of a current unresolved defect. The complete current [GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/mobile-dev-inc/Maestro/issues) is larger than the representative set above; an exhaustive resolution claim is not supported.
