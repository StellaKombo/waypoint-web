How Waypoint handles your data

Developer release · updated October11,2026

Testing on your computer

The Chrome companion observes and controls only the app tab you authorize, with separate permissions for inspection, screenshots and interaction. Sign into your test app yourself. Waypoint does not collect typed passwords, cookies, storage values or network request bodies. Visible page text, screenshots and app logs can still contain private information.

Local evidence

Your browser retains project details, normalized reports and journeys at the dashboard origin. Screenshots and recreation captures remain in memory unless you export them. Exported files can contain app content; review them before sharing. Local storage is not encrypted. Clear local evidence on shared computers.

Account workspace

On an account-enabled server, we store your username, a salted password hash, hashed session tokens, recovery-code hash, projects and the evidence you explicitly save. Screenshots and replay results are excluded from online saves. URL query strings and fragments are removed and common secrets are redacted, but ordinary text can contain personal information. Only save evidence you are allowed to store.

Saved records remain until you delete them or delete the account. Account deletion removes your projects, records and sessions from the active database. Cancel an outstanding subscription through billing first. The operator must publish its backup retention and security/support contact before opening this release to paying users; these operational arrangements are not yet established.

Chosen agents and REA

Your selected agent and its model provider may receive observations and screenshots when you request agent tools. Their data policies apply. The original REA tools run in your separately configured local environment and have their own provider and file access; Waypoint's selected-tab permission does not restrict all REA capabilities.

Payments and hosting

When configured, Stripe hosts Checkout and the billing portal. Card details are entered there, not into Waypoint. Waypoint stores minimal customer/subscription references and entitlement state. Stripe retains its own payment records. The hosting provider processes the server's requests, storage and operational logs. No production hosting or payment acceptance has been verified for this release.

Your controls

You can stop or unpair the companion, export evidence, delete individual online records/projects and delete your account from Account & workspace. Keep your once-displayed recovery code securely; there is no email reset service. The app includes no advertising trackers.
